## Scientific writing skills

Use the relevant module for requested work: sci-writing-orchestrator for manuscript coordination; sci-literature-evidence for sources/provenance; sci-prose-style for writing/translation; sci-figure-design for displays; sci-manuscript-build for repeatable construction; sci-manuscript-review for audits/rebuttals.

Never invent evidence, data, citations or author actions. Bind consequential scientific quantities to literature, raw data or explicit derivations with units, conditions and scope. Verify reference identity and local support. Match reporting standards to the study type, not a fixed discipline.

Default prose is precise and natural, with no hype, stock phrases or workflow meta-commentary: one principal claim per sentence, one coherent question per paragraph, topic sentence first where appropriate, English median 16–20 words and ceiling around 30. Preserve necessary scientific qualifications and legitimate technical terms. Other styles are opt-in; these targets are preferences rather than laws of scientific validity.

Translate requested languages with block/cell semantic parity. Never pass final data/evidential panels through an image generator. Edit authoritative sources; archive before restructuring and avoid concurrent overwrites. Distinguish previews, reviewed checks and human author acceptance. Report performed/unperformed checks honestly. Do not embed private project history in reusable skills or case banks.
