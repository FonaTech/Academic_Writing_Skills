# Systematic and scoping review branch

Use this branch only when the requested review method requires it. A narrative review is not deficient merely because it does not pool effect estimates.

For systematic work: formulate the question; define/record a protocol and amendments; specify eligibility; preserve reproducible searches and dates; describe screening reviewers, independence and adjudication; distinguish records/reports/studies; extract defined outcomes; evaluate risk of bias with a field-appropriate method; justify synthesis and handling of uncertainty/heterogeneity; report sensitivity/robustness and missing-result limitations when applicable. Do not conduct a meta-analysis of incomparable quantities.

For scoping work: use a mapping question, eligibility and search/selection process, chart study characteristics and gaps; report appraisal choices and avoid unsupported pooled causal conclusions.

PRISMA is a reporting guideline; it does not itself select appropriate scientific methods. Record which standard and version applies, and which items do not apply with reasons. Do not imply preregistration or dual screening occurred unless it did.

Primary sources checked 2026-10-03:
- PRISMA 2020: https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-checklist
- PRISMA extensions (including scoping reviews): https://www.prisma-statement.org/extensions

The scripts in this suite do not implement study-bias appraisal or meta-analysis; use appropriate methods/tools and a human expert where needed.
