# Literature search

Choose databases and terminology appropriate to the question and discipline. Write topics/synonyms, dates, study types, inclusion/exclusion criteria and source priorities before a substantial search. Avoid universal non-English exclusions or default citation-count filters. Save full expressions, dates and raw responses.

OpenAlex, Crossref and Semantic Scholar are useful broad sources; add field-specific indexes, registries or archives where needed. Existing `search_openalex.py` supports broad discovery, not exhaustive coverage certification. Combine backward/forward citation chasing and targeted searches for contrary results and replication.

Deduplicate by identifier and title/author/version. Link preprint, accepted and published versions as one study where appropriate. Keep excluded candidates and reasons; distinguish title/abstract from full-text screening. A downloaded PDF requires identity checking and inspection for supplements, truncated text and wrong versions. An inaccessible paper remains an access limitation, not an excluded negative finding.

Narrative reviews state selection scope and limitations. Systematic/scoping reviews use an explicit protocol and their applicable reporting/methods branch. No database order or screening threshold guarantees comprehensiveness. Repeat/update the search near final submission as needed and record the cutoff.
