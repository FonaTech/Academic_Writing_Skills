# Citation audit

Verify publication identity, identifier, title, authors, cited version and bibliographic metadata against the registry or primary publication. Follow the venue's date convention; do not impose print year on every field. A near title match needs inspection, not automatic verification.

Read the exact supporting passage/table/figure. Check that the local claim preserves its objects, methods, conditions, scope, uncertainty and attribution. Secondary sources are labeled; trace consequential primary results to the original when possible. Check relevant publication updates and record access/check dates; absence of a registry flag does not prove no correction exists.

Wrong-paper citations are removed or reassigned to an actually checked source. Missing full text remains an access limitation. Metadata validation does not establish claim support, study quality or truth.
