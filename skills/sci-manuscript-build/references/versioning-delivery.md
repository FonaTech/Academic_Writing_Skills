# Versioning and delivery

Archive authoritative source, bibliography, evidence/data/code, figures, built outputs and review records before restructuring or releasing a reviewed version. Use an explicit version label and preserve delivered snapshots. Avoid overwriting a source owned or currently edited by another collaborator.

Revision notes identify scientific/structural/prose/display changes, corrected facts and citations with their source/provenance, actual new analyses and unresolved items. Distinguish source identity checks, scientific review, build checks, visual inspection and human acceptance.

Deliver requested formats/languages only. Final construction checks require current files and real scoped review records; render and inspect every final page. Figure provenance is matched to the actual artifact type—schematic prompts are unnecessary for a figure drawn entirely in conventional software. Include source and build/review reports when useful for continued revision.

## Requested length variants
Create a separate source directory for each requested length variant, keeping stable semantic block IDs where the same claim appears. Set per-section/body budgets from the user's request and venue rules; do not assume that captions, boxes or tables are universally excluded from a journal's word count. Store a correction ledger with finding, canonical correction, affected blocks, variants/languages, source/evidence check and rebuild status.

Apply factual corrections to all affected variants, then rebuild/check them together. A shorter version may omit evidence detail but must preserve the scope, direction and uncertainty of any retained claim. Keep captions and artwork labels consistent after renumbering; the builder cannot change numbers baked into images.

Before DOCX writes the builder checks for ~$<filename>.docx beside every planned output. On a lock, close the document or choose a new output/project path. This is a lock-file safeguard, not a guarantee of cross-process ownership; inspect parallel work and avoid overwriting another session's source.
