# Versioning and delivery

Archive authoritative source, bibliography, evidence/data/code, figures, built outputs and review records before restructuring or releasing a reviewed version. Use an explicit version label and preserve delivered snapshots. Avoid overwriting a source owned or currently edited by another collaborator.

Revision notes identify scientific/structural/prose/display changes, corrected facts and citations with their source/provenance, actual new analyses and unresolved items. Distinguish source identity checks, scientific review, build checks, visual inspection and human acceptance.

Deliver requested formats/languages only. Final construction checks require current files and real scoped review records; render and inspect every final page. Figure provenance is matched to the actual artifact type—schematic prompts are unnecessary for a figure drawn entirely in conventional software. Include source and build/review reports when useful for continued revision.
