# Referee simulation

Choose complementary perspectives from the actual field and study: domain significance/interpretation, design and methods, evidence/data/replication, and communication. A qualitative study may need an interpretive-methods perspective; theory needs a proof/assumptions reviewer. Do not hardcode a materials/device/system panel for every manuscript.

Give each simulated reviewer the manuscript and exact evidence/source/data access. Reports identify scope, summary, supported strengths, numbered major/minor findings with locations, rationale, feasible repair and uncertainty. No invented credentials or claims of human expert review are allowed.

Independent refutation checks serious findings against actual sources. Separate absent evidence from an affirmative error. Merge actions and identify checks that could not be performed. Simulation helps identify weaknesses; it does not predict an editor's decision or replace author acceptance.
