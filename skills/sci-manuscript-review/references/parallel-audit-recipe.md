# Independent audit recipe

Use bounded independent reviewers/agents when authorized and available. Divide source/data review, attribution and methods, argument, display/translation and document inspection. Sequential passes are acceptable; disclose their independence limit. Multiple agents of the same model do not create an independent expert benchmark.

Provide the exact source, evidence/data/code, bibliography and current artifacts. Findings contain location, actual claim, source locator, reasoning, severity, proposed repair and uncertainty. Verify serious findings directly and seek refutation. Failure to refute is not proof; distinguish missing evidence from an affirmative error.

Apply authorized repairs in source, rebuild and repeat affected checks. A review-only request returns findings. Record actual reviewer identity/coverage and identify AI review as AI. Human author acceptance remains separate.
