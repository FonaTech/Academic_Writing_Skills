# Readiness rubric

Score each criterion 0–4 (1 and 3 lie between the described anchors) and report the total out of 40 with the blocking count. A manuscript with any open blocking finding is **not ready**, whatever its score.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Argument | no clear main claim | claim stated, sections drift | one claim, every section serves it |
| 2 | Evidence accuracy | several wrong numbers or attributions | a few minor mismatches | every checked value and attribution correct |
| 3 | Evidence levels and scope | measured and simulated mixed silently | labelled in tables only | labelled everywhere, scopes stated |
| 4 | Comparison fairness | rankings across scopes | some caveats | like-for-like or normalised with notes |
| 5 | Coverage | key work or comparators missing | main work covered | comprehensive and balanced, gaps stated at field level |
| 6 | Structure | repetition, derivations in text | mostly clean | one argument per section, boxes for derivations |
| 7 | Prose and voice (D6, D11) | hype, staccato chains, defensive sentences, screening-report or fault-finding voice | occasional issues | linked, specific, expert register; a review narrates the field's progress |
| 8 | Terminology | inconsistent terms and definitions | a few slips | fixed terms, defined once, captions self-contained |
| 9 | Figures and tables | errors in artwork or data | correct but weakly integrated | each figure argues one point, data sourced, captions complete |
| 10 | Consistency and build (D9, D10, D12) | numbering, language or variant drift | minor drift | checks pass, both languages and all length variants aligned, references verified |

Interpretation: 36–40 submit; 30–35 one revision round; 20–29 structural revision; below 20 rewrite the plan first.
