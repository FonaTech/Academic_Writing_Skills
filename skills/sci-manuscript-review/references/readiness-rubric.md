# Internal readiness decision

Use three explicit statuses: draft with identified gaps; revision required with open findings; reviewed files ready for author acceptance. Any unresolved blocking fact, method or data-integrity finding prevents a submission-ready status.

Evaluate provenance, evidential support, methods, argument, comparisons, uncertainty, displays, terminology, language and delivery. List actual checks and their limits. Optional internal scores may help prioritize work but are uncalibrated and never predict acceptance or scientific validity. Authors retain final responsibility.
