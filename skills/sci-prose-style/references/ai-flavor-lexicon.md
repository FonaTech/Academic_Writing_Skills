# Removing formulaic prose

Judge the passage's reasoning, not a detector score or a list of common words. Look for generic openings, unsupported praise, interchangeable implications, repeated paragraph templates, mechanical connectors, vague subjects and conclusions detached from evidence.

| Pattern | Repair | Legitimate exception |
|---|---|---|
| “This groundbreaking study unlocks new possibilities” | State the actual result, comparison and scope | A supported priority claim still requires a documented search |
| “Furthermore... Moreover... Importantly...” at every opening | Make each relationship clear through subject/order; keep needed connectors | A real contrast or inference may benefit from an explicit connector |
| “This highlights the crucial importance of...” | Name the consequence and why the evidence supports it | Do not remove a justified substantive conclusion |
| Long clause chain | Split distinct claims and retain conditions | A precise definition or short logical dependency may stay together |
| Uniform short fragments | Recombine naturally related reasoning | A compact abstract may be intentionally concise |

Do not ban technical meanings: credible intervals in Bayesian statistics, boundary conditions in mathematics, significant figures in metrology, or latent features in machine learning. Default precise style uses contextual diagnostic warnings. Editorial polishing cannot change a weak study into a strong one.
