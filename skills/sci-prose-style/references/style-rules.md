# Default precise scientific style

Default profile: precise. Keep natural expert prose, one main claim per sentence and one coherent question per paragraph. Put the topic sentence first; preserve a necessary scientific qualification when restructuring. Prefer concrete subjects and verbs, evidence-bearing statements and explicit scientific relationships.

For English, target a sentence-length median of 16–20 words and a ceiling around 30. These are editorial targets, not scientific thresholds. Retain necessary conditions, uncertainty and technical precision; split or restructure a sentence rather than deleting them. Avoid a choppy series of fragments written only to satisfy a word limit. Chinese is reviewed using meaning and natural Chinese syntax, not English word counts.

Avoid hype, generic promises, repeated transition templates, vague importance claims and commentary about the act of writing. Vary sentence patterns when the argument requires it. Keep legitimate terms such as credible interval, boundary condition, significant association and feature learning; a word alone does not establish “AI flavor.” Do not use AI-detector scores as acceptance criteria or promise that prose cannot be detected as AI-assisted.

Define abbreviations at first relevant use and independently in captions where needed. Keep one term/spelling per concept. Preserve distinctions between measured, modeled, derived, projected and interpreted results. Translation retains object, direction, negation, uncertainty, conditions, values and references.

Available profiles are precise (default), compact, explanatory and generic. They change phrasing/diagnostic targets only, never evidence or scientific rigor. Configure style.profile in manuscript.json, or prose_lint.py --profile NAME. Project overrides remain available. A linter warning is reviewed in context; it cannot certify expert quality or semantic correctness.
