# Case 04: polishing one paragraph, pass by pass

A constructed example with placeholders. It shows the order of the passes, not field content.

## Before (about 100 words, 10 sentences, median 10 words)

> Real systems contain losses. A shared line carries the sum of many signals. Its level therefore decreases with distance. Far units see a smaller input than near units. The error then depends on position. ⟨Subject⟩ adds further losses. These are large. Noise also matters. It limits resolution. Several works study this [5–19].

Problems: three topics in one paragraph (shared-line loss, subject-specific loss, noise); a staccato chain; "therefore" as rhythm; no number anywhere; a range citation that supports nothing specifically; the subject-specific point buried in sentence six.

## Pass 1: argument
Split into three paragraphs: why real systems depart from the ideal, what ⟨subject⟩ adds, and what sets the noise floor. Move any derivation into a box.

## Pass 2: evidence
Find the sourced numbers that make the subject-specific point concrete, with the conditions under which each was measured and the page that reports it. Replace the range citation with one citation per claim.

## Passes 3–5: paragraph, sentence, lexicon
Topic sentence first and naming the point; old information before new; one claim per sentence, linked by a shared subject; drop "therefore"; name what each number measures; no defensive closing.

## After (second of the three paragraphs, shape only)

> ⟨Subject⟩ adds two losses that ⟨reference system⟩ does not have (Fig. 1c). The first is ⟨loss A⟩, about ⟨value⟩ per ⟨unit⟩ under ⟨condition⟩ [23]. A ⟨design variant⟩ lowers it to about ⟨value⟩, against a limit of ⟨value⟩ set by ⟨principle⟩ [23]. The second is ⟨loss B⟩, which dominates once ⟨condition⟩ [24]. Together they set ⟨metric⟩, so ⟨design rule⟩ follows directly; Section ⟨n⟩ takes up the noise that remains.

Profile: five sentences, median about 19 words, every number sourced and conditioned, one figure panel cited, an ending that states the design rule and bridges to the next paragraph.

## Pass 7: second language and variants
The Chinese block carries the same numbers in the same form and the same citation keys; the checker confirms parity. The same paragraph in the shorter variants receives the same factual fixes.
