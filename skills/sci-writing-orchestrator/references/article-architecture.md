# Original research architecture

Start from the actual question, design and evidence. State what was measured or simulated, how it was analyzed, uncertainty, relevant controls and what the results support. Avoid treating the desired main claim as predetermined.

Follow the venue's article structure. IMRaD is a useful default in many empirical fields; mathematical, qualitative and theoretical papers can require different forms. Order findings by their evidential dependencies and reader's question, not a mandatory device-to-system ladder.

Maintain links from raw files and analysis to reported results and display items. Include applicable data/code availability and ethical/reporting requirements. Explain alternative interpretations and limits; a plausible mechanism diagram does not replace a discriminating test. Proposed experiments remain proposed.
