# Original research architecture

Start from the actual question, design and evidence. State what was measured or simulated, how it was analyzed, uncertainty, relevant controls and what the results support. Avoid treating the desired main claim as predetermined.

Follow the venue's article structure. IMRaD is a useful default in many empirical fields; mathematical, qualitative and theoretical papers can require different forms. Order findings by their evidential dependencies and reader's question, not a mandatory device-to-system ladder.

Maintain links from raw files and analysis to reported results and display items. Include applicable data/code availability and ethical/reporting requirements. Explain alternative interpretations and limits; a plausible mechanism diagram does not replace a discriminating test. Proposed experiments remain proposed.

## Section roles
The introduction moves from the concrete research problem to the unresolved question and this study's actual contribution. Explain what prior approaches can establish before identifying the remaining issue. Do not insert headline numbers before they are supported by the results and their conditions.

Order Results by the dependencies of the argument. Each subsection states a finding, the evidence/control that establishes it, uncertainty and the resulting inference. For every figure panel, explain what the reader can conclude. Distinguish a demonstrated mechanism from an interpretation that fits the data.

The Discussion answers the original question, compares the finding at a common scope, evaluates alternatives and states limits that affect generalization. Methods give enough detail for replication and the applicable reporting standard. Captions and tables define abbreviations independently; their numbers and qualifiers match the body and abstract.
