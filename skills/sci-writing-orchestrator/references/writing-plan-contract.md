# Writing plan

For a large new manuscript or structural revision, keep a short living plan: question/type/audience; source ownership and constraints; confirmed decisions and assumptions; proposed section questions and argument; supporting and contrary evidence; raw-data/analysis needs; figure plan; languages/output formats; milestones and review scope; open items and definition of done.

Revise it when evidence or author decisions change. Do not create a large planning artifact for a bounded edit. A plan records authorized work and does not imply publication, third-party messaging or new experiments were approved.
