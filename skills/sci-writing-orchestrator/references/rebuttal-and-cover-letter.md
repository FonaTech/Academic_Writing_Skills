# Rebuttals and cover letters

The full guidance lives in the review skill: `sci-manuscript-review/references/rebuttal-letters.md`. This page is the orchestrator's checklist.

1. Collect the decision letter and every referee report; number the comments (R1.1, R1.2, …).
2. Classify each comment: correction, new analysis, clarification, disagreement, out of scope.
3. Plan the changes in the writing plan (section 6 and 8 updates) before editing text.
4. Apply changes in the manuscript source; rebuild; keep a tracked-changes copy if the journal asks for one.
5. Draft the response letter from the plan: comment quoted, response, change with location.
6. Check that every promised change exists in the rebuilt manuscript; check numbers again.
7. Cover letter for the resubmission: what changed in three to five points, and thanks in one sentence.
