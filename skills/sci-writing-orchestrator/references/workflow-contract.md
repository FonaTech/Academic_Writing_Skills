# Ten-step contract

Scale depth to the task. A draft, structural revision and submission package have different completion conditions. Steps can loop and overlap; preserve scientific dependencies.

| Step | Input and actions | Output | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Question/scope | Select field, study type, task-specific professional role, reader, research question, scope, venue, default/selected style, languages and deliverables; separate observations from hypotheses | Brief with roles, assumptions and provisional claims | Scope is usable; unresolved decisions that change the work are explicit |
| 2 Material audit | Identify authoritative source; inventory drafts, datasets, scripts, figures, literature and prior decisions; detect conflicts and concurrent work | Inventory, gap list, source/version snapshot | Data ownership and edit target are clear |
| 3 Literature | Choose databases and search expressions; save dates/results; record inclusion/exclusion and contrary evidence; check study versions | Search log, selection records and curated library | Coverage fits the mode; limitations and inaccessible sources are recorded |
| 4 Evidence | Literature: full quote, page and identity; original results: files, hashes, locators and methods; derived: formula, inputs and conditions; record uncertainty | Evidence records and checked bibliography | Every consequential claim has support or an explicit pending label; verification scope is stated |
| 5 Argument/text | Outline section questions, supporting/contrary evidence and limits; draft small sections; revise scientific reasoning before language | Section sources with stable IDs, claim bindings and revision status | Claims follow evidence; alternatives and qualifications survive rewriting |
| 6 Displays | Specify figure/table purpose and data; code-draw data; create independent schematics; write captions; check figure/text agreement | Assets, plotting code, provenance, captions and composition records | Values and scopes match; final data panels never pass through a generator |
| 7 Languages | If requested, translate by block and table cell; verify quantities/units, qualifiers, polarity, evidence state and references | Paired sources and terminology | Semantic equivalence reviewed; token checks pass but are not the only review |
| 8 Construction | Build requested variants from content/metadata; preview during drafting; refine layout after content stabilizes; render every final page | Documents, build hashes and rendered pages | Outputs exist, source is current, numbering correct, all pages inspected |
| 9 Independent review | Review facts, support, methods, comparisons, alternatives, figures and language; refute serious findings; repair source and repeat affected checks | Findings, fixes and scoped review records | No unresolved blocking findings; self-review/AI review accurately identified |
| 10 Acceptance | Author reviews conclusions, limitations, policies and unresolved items; archive reviewed source/output version | Accepted or explicitly labeled draft deliverable | Status is honest; author retains scientific and submission responsibility |

Do not turn all drafts into systematic reviews. Do not force bilingual output. Do not claim unperformed reviews or missing renders are complete. No shell success code can establish scientific validity.
