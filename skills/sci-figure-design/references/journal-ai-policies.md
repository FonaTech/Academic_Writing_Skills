# Check the actual venue policy

Generative-image and AI-disclosure rules can change and may differ among journals owned by the same publisher. Check the current target-journal author guidance before final artwork/submission and save the URL, access date, relevant rule and workflow/disclosure decision. Do not infer permission from another publisher's policy or from a successful image-integrity check.

Primary starting points: [Nature Portfolio AI policy](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai), [Wiley author AI guidance](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/publish/article/ai-guidelines/). Both pages could not be independently re-fetched in this validation environment on 2026-10-03; no current blanket permission/prohibition is asserted here. Check the specific journal, including AAAS/Science journals, directly.

The suite's data-integrity rule is independent of publisher permission: do not pass final data panels, micrographs, diagnostic/evidential images or results through an image generator. For conceptual schematics, retain a design/provenance record; use conventional drawing tools where required. If an AI-created final is permitted, follow the exact disclosure and copyright requirements. Redrawing does not automatically remove every disclosure or rights obligation.

For reused or adapted published panels record the source, original figure/panel, actual licence or permission, modifications and required credit. CC BY, other licences and written permission have different conditions. Keep a sources.csv or equivalent record. A machine-generated licence label alone does not establish permission.
